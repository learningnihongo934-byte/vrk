Honest answers.

Why a command instead of a download on Mac?

Downloaded Mac apps need a paid Apple certificate, or macOS calls them "damaged". The command downloads VRK, clears that flag and signs it for your Mac, so it just opens. You can read the script first: vrkweb.web.app/install.sh.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

That's SmartScreen warning about any app without a paid code-signing certificate. Click More info → Run anyway, or use the PowerShell command, which doesn't trigger it.

What do I need?

A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer), Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). About 600 MB of space. Linux is coming soon.

Is Tor in VRK as safe as Tor Browser?

No. Tor tabs hide your address from sites and are great for everyday privacy, but if your safety depends on anonymity, use Tor Browser.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no data collection. You can read the source on GitHub; copying or redistributing VRK isn’t allowed (see the license).
